## Rules
- "Free download" does **not** mean "free for commercial use". Read the licence page and save a copy of it to `assets/_licences/`.
- Preferred: CC0 / public domain, then CC-BY (attribution in credits), OFL for fonts.
- Not allowed: NC (non-commercial), ND (no derivatives) where we modify, ripped or unknown-source assets.
- Paid assets require the owner's approval first.
